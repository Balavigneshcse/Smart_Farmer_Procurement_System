How it works

  1. Register with your email

    Log in with an OTP sent to your email. No password to remember.

  2. Book a time slot

    Choose your centre, date, time and crop. You get a token number.

  3. Come at your time

    See your queue position and waiting time live. We email you when your turn is near.

    Your crop is weighed and checked, and you can track the payment until it is credited.
